Recommended: finding, on the wide phone

Search becomes a find bar right above the keyboard, like Safari's Find on Page: the field, "3 of 12", the result arrows and Done, together where the thumbs already are. The top bar steps aside while it is open, so the transcript runs from the top of the screen down to the find bar: about 173pt, against 136pt today. Nothing lies over the handle. When the keyboard closes, the find bar stays at the bottom with its count and arrows until Done.
