The owner's idea: the toolbar at the bottom, in a pill

The pattern iOS 26 uses everywhere: Safari's own bottom bar (a back circle and a pill with share, reload and compass), and the floating tab bar with a separate search circle in Mail, Notes, Music and Photos. Controls float in a glass capsule inset from the edges and above the home indicator; the content scrolls under them; search sits at the bottom and grows into a field above the keyboard. Here the phone's second top row moves into that pill, so the top keeps one row, and the find bar from the frames above becomes the pill's search state.
